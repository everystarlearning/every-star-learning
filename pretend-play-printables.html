Café & supermarket pretend-play printables

Turn a small corner into a café or a little shop. Explore printable role-play kits with British play money and room for your child’s own ideas.

Choose your printable

My Little Café: 25 A4 pages

Build a café scene with a priced menu, food and drink cut-outs, plates, bowls and cutlery. The PDF also includes tick-box order forms, receipts, a printable cash register, British-inspired play notes and coins, loyalty cards, role tags and open/closed signs. A two-page awning and parent/carer guide complete the set.

My Little Supermarket: 24 A4 pages

The supermarket PDF includes 112 grocery cut-outs across seven departments, with matching shelves, two shopping baskets and a trolley. There is also a printable register, barcode scanner, pretend debit cards, British-inspired play money, a two-page awning and a parent/carer guide.

Print, prepare and play

Both kits are sold separately as one full-colour PDF each. Print at 100% actual size on good-quality white card, then cut out the pieces. Laminating is optional. Join the awning pages and attach them to a cardboard box, wall or play stand.

The listings recommend ages 3–11 according to interests and abilities, with adult supervision for cutting, laminating and small pieces. Neither kit is suitable for children under 3. Supermarket cleaning-product illustrations are pretend props: never use real cleaning products for play.

Choose a few pieces to start and take turns being the customer or shopkeeper. See our SEND home education activity ideas for more ways to begin.

A few useful answers

Is the play money British?

Both kit listings describe British play money. It is for pretend play, not real currency.

Do I need to buy both kits?

No. The café and supermarket are separate digital products. Choose either setting, or explore both.

Is this a physical product?

No. These are digital downloads to print yourself. The photographs show product previews or examples of the finished activity.

How do I get the files?
