Printable phonics, alphabet & number cards

Small cards. Lots to explore. Choose alphabet matching, letter sounds, blending words or numbers 1–20 to suit what your child is working on.

Choose your printable

Download through Etsy · Print at home · No physical item posted

Alphabet matching: letters and pictures

Match 26 lowercase letter cards to 26 picture cards. The download contains pink and blue versions as two PDFs, with 14 A4 pages per colour. Print on paper or card, cut out the cards and optionally laminate them.

Phonics: 31 sound cards

One PDF contains 31 cards with a letter or sound, visual cue and example word. Examples in the listing include s as in sun and sh as in ship. The seller suggests card or optional lamination; this listing does not specify a paper size.

Blending: 48 word cards

Twelve A4 pages contain four cards each, with sound dots beneath the words. The set starts with simple CVC words and includes more challenging blends and sounds. Point to each dot, say the sounds and blend the word, beginning with a few cards at a time.

Counting: number cards 1–20

Five A4 pages contain 20 cards, four per page. Each card combines a numeral, its written number word and matching counting dots. Print on paper or card and cut out the cards; lamination is optional.

Choose the set you need

These are four separate digital products. Alphabet, blending and number-card listings specify personal/educational use; the phonics listing specifies personal/home educational use. Do not assume that buying a pack permits redistribution of its files. Follow the individual Etsy listing’s terms.

For reading practice, follow your child’s existing phonics approach where relevant. These cards are resources to use alongside your plans, not a complete reading programme.
