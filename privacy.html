Privacy & cookies

Updated 26 November 2026.

Your choice

This website works without optional analytics. Google Analytics loads only after you select “Accept analytics”. Rejecting it does not affect browsing or buying through Etsy. Use to change your choice at any time.

What analytics measures

If you agree, we use Google Analytics to understand page visits and clicks to our Etsy shop. It receives technical information such as browser and device details, page addresses and a cookie identifier. Google processes connection information, including your IP address, to provide the service. Our Etsy click labels identify the website page, button placement and public listing ID; a click is not a confirmed purchase.

Our website tracking does not intentionally send names, email addresses, payment details or information about your child. We do not enable Google signals or advertising personalisation in the website tag. Read Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and browser storage

We store your accept or reject choice in this browser for up to 180 days. If you accept, Google Analytics can set cookies beginning _ga with a configured lifetime of up to 180 days. Rejecting analytics removes these Analytics cookies where accessible and stops future website analytics. It does not delete information already processed by Google.

For our own testing, a separate staff-browser preference can prevent analytics entirely. That preference stays in that browser until switched off or browser storage is cleared. It is not an account or a login.

Orders and other websites

Orders, payments, messages and digital downloads are handled by Etsy, under Etsy’s privacy policy. Links to TikTok and Google have their own privacy terms. We do not receive your payment-card details through this website.

Questions

Contact Every Star Learning at claire@everystarlearning.co.uk for questions about this website’s use of data. For an Etsy order, use Etsy’s order or messaging tools.
