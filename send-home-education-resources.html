SEND Home Education Resources UK ⭐

Personalised learning resources designed to support children with SEND who learn at home.

Every child learns differently. At Every Star Learning, we create personalised SEND learning packs around each child's age, interests and individual learning needs, helping families make home learning more flexible, engaging and manageable.

Supporting SEND Through Home Education

Home education can give families more freedom to shape learning around a child's individual needs, strengths, interests and pace.

For some children with SEND, ADHD, autism or additional learning needs, this flexibility can make learning feel less pressured and more accessible.

Every Star Learning resources are designed to support that flexibility with personalised, practical and engaging activities.

Why Flexible Learning Can Help

Some children may find traditional learning environments difficult because of sensory needs, concentration, communication differences, anxiety, transitions or the pace of the day.

Home learning can allow families to adapt around those needs.

  • Learn at a pace that suits the child
  • Take breaks when needed
  • Use interests to encourage engagement
  • Reduce unnecessary pressure
  • Adapt activities around energy and concentration
  • Use practical, visual and sensory resources
  • Revisit activities as often as needed

Personalised SEND Home Education Packs

Every Star Learning packs are designed around the individual child rather than using a one-size-fits-all approach.

Personalisation can take into account your child's age, interests, preferred activities and individual learning needs.

The aim is to provide useful resources that feel relevant and manageable for your child and your family's learning routine.

What Can Be Included?

Our featured personalised SEND learning pack can include:

  • Age-appropriate educational workbooks
  • Reusable personalised learning activities
  • Sensory and fidget resources
  • Emotion and communication cards
  • A reward chart and stickers
  • Colouring pencils and writing equipment
  • Outdoor nature-finding activities
  • The Every Star Learning Parent Guide

Learning Through a Child's Interests

A child's interests can be a valuable starting point for learning.

Whether they enjoy animals, transport, sport, nature, space, characters, numbers or another subject, familiar interests can help activities feel more meaningful and enjoyable.

This is one of the reasons personalisation is such an important part of Every Star Learning.

Supporting Different Learning Styles

Some children learn best through reading and writing, while others respond better to visual, practical or hands-on activities.

Our packs combine different types of resources so families can choose the activities that work best for their child.

There is no expectation that every activity must be completed in the same way or in a particular order.

SEND Resources for Different Ages

Our current learning packs are designed around four age groups:

  • Little Stars: ages 3–5
  • Rising Stars: ages 5–7
  • Shooting Stars: ages 7–9
  • Super Stars: ages 9–11

Age is a useful starting point, but every child develops differently. Personalisation helps us take account of the child's individual stage, interests and learning needs.

Building Confidence at Home

Learning difficulties can sometimes affect a child's confidence, particularly if they have repeatedly found certain activities difficult or overwhelming.

Our aim is to help create positive learning experiences through manageable activities, encouragement and opportunities to celebrate progress.

Children can work at their own pace, revisit familiar activities and take breaks whenever they need them.

Can the Packs Be Used Alongside Other Home Education Resources?

Yes. Every Star Learning packs are designed to complement a family's wider home education approach.

They can be used alongside online learning, books, educational visits, practical activities, tutors or any other resources that suit your child.

Our packs are not intended to be a complete curriculum.

Can the Packs Be Used if My Child Still Attends School?

Yes. The resources can also be used by families whose children attend school.

Activities can be used at home, at weekends or during school holidays as additional learning support without needing to turn home time into extra formal schoolwork.

Every Child Is Different

SEND covers a wide range of needs, and no single approach will work for every child.

Every Star Learning focuses on flexibility and personalisation so families can choose resources and activities that feel right for their child.

Every Star Learning resources are educational materials and do not diagnose or treat any condition. They are not intended to replace advice, assessments or support from qualified education, health or therapy professionals.
