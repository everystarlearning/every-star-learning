Print, prepare and play

Download the PDF after your Etsy payment is confirmed. Print at 100% actual size on good-quality white A4 card, then cut out the pieces. Lamination is optional. Join the two awning pages and attach them to a cardboard box, wall or play stand.

Adult supervision is recommended for cutting, laminating and small pieces. Not suitable for children under 3. Never use real cleaning products as play accessories.

A simple way to start

Start with a few groceries and sort them onto the matching shelves. Take turns choosing items and being the cashier. You can count a small basket together, or simply point to the items you want.

There is no need to use every piece at once. Choose a small selection and follow your child’s interests.
