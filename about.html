About Me

I’m a home-educating mum, and Every Star Learning grew from my own experience of supporting my neurodivergent children at home.

When I first began home educating, I quickly realised how much time went into finding suitable resources, gathering materials and working out what suited each child. It could feel overwhelming before the learning had even begun.

That’s why I created Every Star Learning. Our packs bring together practical learning resources, creative activities and supportive tools in one place, helping to take some of that pressure away from parents.

My aim is simple: to make home learning a little easier for families and help every child learn, grow and shine in their own way. ⭐
