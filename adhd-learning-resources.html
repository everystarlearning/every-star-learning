A small activity to try together

Choose a favourite subject and one short activity, with a clear place to stop. For a child who enjoys vehicles, you could count three toy cars, sort them by colour, then choose whether to carry on or take a break. Adapt the length and materials to your child; stopping is an option too.

Use this as a starting point, rather than a task your child has to finish.

Supporting Children with ADHD Through Learning

Children with ADHD may find some traditional learning approaches difficult. Long activities, repetitive tasks, distractions, organisation and staying focused for extended periods can sometimes make learning feel overwhelming.

That does not mean a child cannot learn successfully. Often, they simply benefit from learning being presented differently.

Flexible activities, movement, visual resources, practical learning and subjects connected to a child's interests can all help make learning more accessible and enjoyable.

ADHD-Friendly Learning

An ADHD-friendly approach can include:

  • Shorter and more manageable activities
  • Clear and simple instructions
  • Hands-on learning
  • Visual learning resources
  • Sensory and fidget resources
  • Movement and regular breaks
  • Reward and motivation systems
  • Activities based around the child's interests
  • Flexible learning at the child's own pace

Learning Through Interests

Interests can be a powerful way to encourage engagement.

If a child loves animals, space, dinosaurs, vehicles, sport or another particular subject, those interests can help make activities feel more meaningful and enjoyable.

This is one of the reasons personalisation is such an important part of Every Star Learning.

Building Confidence

Children who regularly find learning difficult can sometimes lose confidence or begin to feel that they are simply not good at learning.

We want to help change that experience.

Our resources are designed to encourage achievable progress, positive reinforcement and learning in a way that feels manageable rather than overwhelming.

ADHD Learning Resources for Home Education

Every Star Learning packs can also support families who home educate.

Home learning can provide greater flexibility around concentration, movement, breaks, interests and daily routines.

Our packs can be used as part of a wider home education approach, alongside other resources, or simply as additional learning activities at home.

Can the Packs Be Used Alongside School?

Yes. Families can also use activities alongside school learning, at weekends or during school holidays.

There is no need to complete everything in one sitting. Activities can be chosen according to your child's energy, interest and concentration.

Every Child Is Different

ADHD does not look the same in every child, and there is no single learning method that will work for everyone.

Our aim is to provide flexible resources that can support different learning styles while recognising each child's strengths, interests and individuality.

Every Star Learning resources are educational materials and do not diagnose or treat ADHD. They are not intended to replace advice, assessments or support from qualified education, health or therapy professionals.
