Autism Learning Resources for Children UK ⭐

Personalised learning resources designed to support autistic children in a way that recognises their individual strengths, interests and learning needs.

Every autistic child is different. At Every Star Learning, we create personalised learning packs around your child's age, interests, preferences and individual learning needs, helping make learning more flexible, engaging and manageable.

Supporting Autistic Children Through Learning

Autistic children can have very different learning styles, interests, strengths and support needs. Some may prefer clear routines and structure, while others may benefit from more flexibility and choice.

Sensory needs, communication preferences, concentration, transitions and the learning environment can all affect how comfortable and engaged a child feels.

A personalised approach can help make learning feel more predictable, meaningful and accessible.

Autism-Friendly Learning

Autism-friendly learning can include:

  • Clear and simple instructions
  • Visual learning resources
  • Predictable and manageable activities
  • Learning through a child's interests
  • Sensory-friendly resources
  • Hands-on activities
  • Choice and flexibility
  • Regular breaks when needed
  • Learning at the child's own pace

Personalised Autism Learning Packs

Every Star Learning packs are designed around the individual child rather than using the same approach for everyone.

Personalisation can take into account your child's age, interests, preferred activities, sensory preferences and individual learning needs.

By creating resources around things a child already enjoys or feels comfortable with, learning can feel more engaging and less pressured.

Learning Through Special Interests

A child's interests can be a valuable part of learning.

Whether a child is interested in animals, transport, numbers, space, characters, nature or another particular subject, those interests can help make activities feel more familiar and motivating.

This is one of the reasons personalisation is an important part of Every Star Learning.

Supporting Communication and Emotional Understanding

Some autistic children may benefit from visual support around emotions, communication and everyday situations.

Our packs can include emotion and communication resources designed to support learning and discussion in a calm and accessible way.

Families can use these resources in whichever way feels most appropriate for their child.

Sensory-Friendly Learning

Sensory preferences can have a big influence on how comfortable a child feels while learning.

Some children may enjoy tactile or sensory resources, while others may prefer fewer distractions or particular textures and activities.

Letting us know about sensory likes or dislikes can help us understand what may be more suitable for your child.

Building Confidence

Learning should not leave a child feeling that they are failing because they approach things differently.

Our aim is to provide resources that encourage achievable progress, positive experiences and confidence.

Children can revisit activities, take breaks and work at a pace that feels manageable for them.

Autism Learning Resources for Home Education

Every Star Learning packs can also support families who home educate.

Home learning can allow families to create routines and activities around a child's individual needs, interests, energy levels and sensory preferences.

Our packs can be used as part of a wider home education approach, alongside other resources, or simply as additional learning activities at home.

Can the Packs Be Used Alongside School?

Yes. Families can use selected resources alongside school learning, at weekends or during school holidays.

There is no need to complete everything in one sitting. Activities can be chosen according to your child's interest, energy and comfort.

Every Autistic Child Is Different

Autism does not look the same in every child, and there is no single learning approach that will suit everyone.

Our aim is to offer flexible resources that recognise individuality and help children learn in a way that feels more comfortable and meaningful to them.

Every Star Learning resources are educational materials and do not diagnose or treat autism. They are not intended to replace advice, assessments or support from qualified education, health or therapy professionals.
