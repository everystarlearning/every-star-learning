Christmas colouring activities that become something to keep

Choose cards to colour, gift tags or Santa door hangers. These printable colouring sheets are designed to become cards, tags and decorations—not a standalone colouring book. The Hello Winter banner adds a larger colour-and-display activity.

Christmas crafts to cut, fold and make

Build a 3D paper gingerbread house, assemble vintage Christmas bunting or complete a handprint keepsake. Ready-coloured options offer an alternative when colouring is not the activity you want.

Planning an Xmas craft afternoon? Choose by activity and materials, then check the exact listing before buying.

Using Christmas activities for learning and play

Colouring, cutting, folding and writing a short card message can be part of supported creative play. These are craft resources; we do not present them as Christmas maths, phonics or curriculum-aligned worksheets. For separate educational resources, browse our phonics and number printables.

An adult can read instructions or prepare smaller pieces. Offer one manageable step at a time and follow the child’s preferences. Age suitability depends on the task, materials and support available.
