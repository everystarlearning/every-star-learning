A few useful answers

Is this a physical product?

No. These are digital downloads to print yourself. The photographs show product previews or examples of the finished activity.

How do I get the files?

Purchase through the linked Etsy listing, then follow Etsy’s download instructions. For help downloading and printing, read our printable FAQs.

What do I need to check before buying?

Check the listing’s file format, paper size, contents and usage terms. A printer, paper and any craft materials are separate from the digital download.
