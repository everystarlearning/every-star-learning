This PDF provides the printable design for a Christmas tree handprint picture. You add your child’s painted handprint yourself. The finished artwork can be displayed or placed in your own frame.

Use a printer, suitable paper or card and paint intended for the child’s age and this activity. Follow the paint manufacturer’s instructions and supervise younger children. Allow the picture to dry fully before handling or framing.
