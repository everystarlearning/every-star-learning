A Little Help for Your Star ⭐

Questions about our personalised SEND learning packs? You’re in the right place.

Choosing resources for your child can feel like a big decision. Here are some helpful answers, so you can explore what might work for your family at your own pace.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a personalised SEND learning pack?

It’s a collection of learning resources tailored to your child’s age, interests and individual learning needs. SEND stands for special educational needs and disabilities.

Our packs bring together structured activities, hands-on learning and sensory resources, with the aim of making learning more flexible and helping your child build confidence at their own pace.

Are the packs suitable for children with ADHD?

Our packs are designed with children with ADHD and additional learning needs in mind. They combine different ways to take part, including hands-on activities and sensory resources.

Every child is different. Tell us about your child’s interests, what helps them engage and anything they find frustrating, so we can discuss whether a pack is a good fit. You can use activities in short sessions and take breaks whenever your child needs them.

Are the packs suitable for autistic children?

Our packs are also designed with autistic children in mind, with personalisation around each child’s interests and learning needs. There isn’t one approach that suits every autistic child.

Let us know about preferred activities, communication needs and sensory likes or dislikes before ordering. We can talk through suitability and what can be personalised for your child.

What ages are the packs for?

Our current age groups are:

  • Little Stars: ages 3–5
  • Rising Stars: ages 5–7
  • Shooting Stars: ages 7–9
  • Super Stars: ages 9–11

Age is a starting point. If your child is between groups or working at different stages in different areas, get in touch through Etsy before ordering so we can help you choose.

How is the pack personalised for my child?

Personalisation is based on your child’s age, interests and individual learning needs. Follow the personalisation instructions in the Etsy listing when placing your order.

Useful details include activities your child enjoys, skills you would like to support and resources they find difficult to use. If you have a particular request, message us through Etsy before buying so we can confirm what’s possible. Share only the information needed to help us understand your child’s learning preferences.

What comes inside a learning pack?

Our featured personalised SEND learning pack includes:

  • Age-appropriate educational workbooks
  • Reusable personalised learning activities
  • Sensory and fidget resources
  • Emotion and communication cards
  • A reward chart and stickers
  • Colouring pencils and writing equipment
  • Outdoor nature-finding activities
  • The Every Star Learning Parent Guide

Check the current Etsy listing for the contents of the pack you’re ordering. If a particular item or sensory preference matters to your child, ask us about it before purchasing.

Can we use a pack for home education?

Yes. Our packs are designed to support flexible home learning and home education. You can choose activities around your child’s interests, revisit favourites and work at a pace that suits your family.

Think of your pack as a set of resources to support your wider learning plans. It isn’t a complete curriculum or an assessment of your child’s progress.

Can we use the activities alongside school?

Yes. You can use selected activities at home alongside school learning, during weekends or in the holidays. There’s no need to complete everything in one sitting or turn it into extra homework.

Follow your child’s energy and interest, and choose what feels manageable for your family.

How long will delivery take?

Please check the current processing time and estimated delivery dates on the Etsy listing before ordering. Preparing a personalised pack takes time, and delivery estimates may vary with your location and when you order.

If you need a pack for a particular date, message us through Etsy before purchasing to check whether that’s possible. For an existing order, check your Etsy order details and contact us there with any questions.

Do the packs replace professional advice or support?

No. Our packs are educational resources. They do not diagnose or treat ADHD, autism or any other condition, and they do not replace advice, assessments or support from qualified education, health or therapy professionals.

Use them alongside the support your child already receives. If you have concerns about your child’s development, health or learning needs, speak with an appropriate qualified professional.
